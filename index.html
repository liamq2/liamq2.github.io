About me

I'm a results-driven IT professional from the east end of Glasgow. I've spent nearly 4 years on the service desk at William Grant & Sons, growing from 1st line technical support into a very broad role spanning security operations work (in line with my security development), identity management, and endpoint management, supporting various projects along the way.
